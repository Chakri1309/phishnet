    Under the hood

    What happens when you press Analyse

    1Lexical sweep

    Checks for @ tricks, dashes in the domain, sneaky redirects, excessive length, shorteners, and raw IP hosts — instantly, no network needed.

    2Page forensics

    If the page loads, PhishNet inspects its forms, iframes, favicons, link targets, and scripts that hide where links really go.

    3Domain intelligence

    DNS resolution, registration age, and blacklist patterns separate throwaway scam domains from established sites.

    4Ensemble verdict

    {{ metrics.best_model }} turns the 30 signals into a risk score — with every signal listed so you can verify the reasoning.
